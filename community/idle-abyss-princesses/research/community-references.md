## Current official Discord constraints verified

- [Community Onboarding FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/11074987197975-Community-Onboarding-FAQ): enabling native onboarding currently requires at least seven Default Channels, at least five of which must let @everyone View and Send Messages. Default channels are seen by all new members; questions can add roles and channels; questions can be required, allow multiple answers, and be marked to appear before join or later in Channels & Roles. The FAQ recommends keeping choices short and avoiding excess options. Those are native product constraints/guidance. Proposed channel set and question order below are our design choices.
- [Community Onboarding Examples](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10394859532823-Community-Onboarding-Examples): Discord recommends not overwhelming people with long questions, suggests about six maximum choices for a primary channel-picking question, and distinguishes pre-join questions from optional later customization. The proposed required language plus optional interests/favorite-character questions is design inference; notification preferences remain explicitly opt-in and separate.
- [Server Guide FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/13497665141655-Server-Guide-FAQ): native Server Guide supports a welcome sign, 3–5 New Member To-Dos, and read-only channels presented as resource pages; converting a read-only channel to a resource page removes it from the channel list. Enabling it means members see Server Guide instead of the Welcome Screen, while Rules Screening still appears. Design choice: use rules, FAQ, and roles/notification instructions as resource pages; keep 👋・欢迎-welcome and 📢・公告-announcements as visible Default Channels and do not convert them. The “say hello” task points to the writable 💬・闲聊-general channel. The five tasks proposed in `onboarding-spec.json` are suggestions, not live settings.
- [Forum Channels FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/6208479917079-Forum-Channels-FAQ): forums are available in Community servers, support post guidelines and tags (including requiring tags), and support AutoMod. Discord's current [Channels API Forum Tag Object](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/channel#forum-tag-object-forum-tag-structure) documents tag names 0–20 characters and `moderated`, which restricts tag addition/removal to members with Manage Threads. An archived Discord Help Center community-feedback post reports 20 tags per forum and five tags per post: [archived community feedback](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/9159408225559-Feedback-on-Forum-tag-limit-and-per-post-tag-limit). This is a historical user report, not current official product documentation or a verified present-day hard limit. The proposal uses these only as conservative planning targets; confirm the live UI/API before forum setup. Proposed status tags use the `moderated` flag; member topic/platform tags do not. Staff-only status-tag use assumes moderators have Manage Threads.
- [Server Banners](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028716472-Server-Banners): a static server banner is available at Boost Level 2 (Discord Partner servers are an exception); Discord recommends 960x540 / 16:9. No boost purchase is proposed. This is a paid/eligibility feature constraint, not a required onboarding element.
- [Setting Up Permissions FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/206029707-Setting-Up-Permissions-FAQ): roles and channel overwrites govern access. All proposed member roles below are cosmetic and opt-in. Do not attach permission grants, staff powers, or private-area access to onboarding answer roles.

## Recommended shape (design inference)

Keep the first screen simple and useful: seven Default Channels, with welcome and announcements read-only and the other five writable to @everyone. This exactly meets the documented 7/5 onboarding minimum while making all seven obviously useful. Offer the international space as an optional language-based addition, not a ninth default. Require only the language answer; interests and favorite characters should be optional, so neither becomes a participation gate. Place update/event/test pings in a separate after-join, unchecked-by-default opt-in question. Never bundle pings with a language or character answer.

Use public forums 🐞・问题反馈-bug-reports and 💡・建议-suggestions under feedback, plus the intended community forum 📘・攻略配装-builds, rather than one channel per character. The feedback area also proposes read-only 📍・已知问题-known-issues and manually permissioned 🔒・私密求助-private-help; do not imply automated tickets. Publish short templates and explain public reports may be reviewed. Give staff status tags moderation-only use. No community/member role should grant staff permissions or access.

Discord calls the four-character profile/community label a Server Tag, distinct from member cosmetic roles, forum tags, and the separate Discord Guilds product. The [Server Tags FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/31444248479639-Server-Tags) says it requires three available Server Boosts as an additional perk independent of server boost level; the [Server Boosting FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028038352-Server-Boosting-FAQ) says the perk needs ongoing allocated boosts. ABYS remains a candidate label only. Availability was not checked; nothing was unlocked, allocated, or purchased.

## Scope and confidence

Public discovery profiles can describe a server's advertised focus and categories; they do not establish its unseen channel permissions, moderation quality, staff behavior, private conversations, or actual member experience. We did not join Milky Way Idle or Melvor Idle, and did not inspect their private channels or chats. Steam snapshots/screenshot are local copies of official public material. This document is evidence for a prepared proposal, not evidence that any proposed onboarding question/forum/tag has been created or enabled in Discord.
