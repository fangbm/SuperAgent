# 💡・建议-suggestions · Suggestions

欢迎提出玩法、界面、可访问性、社区或其他改进建议。发帖前搜索是否已有相同主题；一个帖子聚焦一个想法。说明想解决的体验问题、建议方案，以及可能的取舍。尊重不同意见；这里是讨论区，不代表建议已被接受或承诺实现。

Suggestions about gameplay, interface, accessibility, community, and other improvements are welcome. Search for an existing topic first and keep each post focused on one idea. Explain the player problem, your proposed change, and possible trade-offs. Disagreement is welcome when respectful. A discussion here does not mean a suggestion was accepted or promised.
