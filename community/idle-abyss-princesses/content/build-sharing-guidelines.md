# 📘・攻略配装-builds · Builds & Guides

欢迎分享装备、技能、天赋、符文、抗性和战斗测试思路。可附上适用内容/难度、角色、关键配置、操作循环、测试环境与优缺点；说明仍在尝试的部分。比较不同流派时保持友善，不把个人结果说成唯一解。截图请避免暴露个人信息。

Share equipment, skills, talents, runes, resistances, and testing ideas. Include the content/difficulty, heroine, key setup, rotation, test conditions, and strengths or trade-offs; label experiments as works in progress. Be respectful when comparing approaches and do not present a personal result as the only answer. Keep personal information out of screenshots.
