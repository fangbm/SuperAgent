# 社区规则 · Community Rules

1. 友善交流。禁止骚扰、仇恨言论、歧视、人肉或威胁；可以批评游戏和提出不同意见，请讨论问题而非攻击玩家或开发者。
2. 本服为全年龄友好空间。禁止色情、露骨内容及其他不适合公开社区的内容。
3. 剧情、结局和关键机制请使用 Discord 剧透标记 `||内容||`，并在明显处标注剧透范围。
4. 禁止刷屏、广告、未经许可的推广、诈骗、钓鱼链接和冒充工作人员。可疑私信请举报给版主。
5. 不要公开发布密码、登录令牌、完整存档、个人资料或他人的隐私。工作人员不会索要密码或令牌。
6. 反馈请保持具体、尊重并使用相应论坛。不要重复发帖或要求其他玩家替你施压。
7. 请遵守 Discord 条款及适用法律。版主可移除内容、限制发言或封禁违规账号；如有误判，可私下礼貌联系版主复核。

1. Be kind. Harassment, hate, discrimination, doxxing, and threats are not allowed. Criticism and disagreement are welcome; discuss the issue without attacking people.
2. Keep this public server suitable for a general audience. No sexual/explicit content or other material unsuitable for a public community.
3. Mark story, ending, and major-mechanic spoilers with Discord spoiler tags `||text||`, and label the spoiler scope clearly.
4. No spam, unsolicited promotion, scams, phishing links, or impersonating staff. Report suspicious DMs to moderators.
5. Never post passwords, login tokens, complete save files, personal data, or someone else's private information. Staff will never ask for your password or token.
6. Keep feedback specific and respectful, and use the matching forum. Do not duplicate posts or organize pressure campaigns against other players.
7. Follow Discord's Terms and applicable law. Moderators may remove content, restrict posting, or ban accounts for violations. If you believe a decision was mistaken, contact a moderator privately and respectfully.
