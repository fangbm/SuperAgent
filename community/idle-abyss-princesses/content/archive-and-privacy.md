# 消息保存与隐私 · Archive & Privacy

归档是本系统的唯一事实来源：先保存消息原文和修订，再从归档读取并分析；摘要和工单只是整理结果。目前本服务器的归档机器人和 AI 处理尚未安装或启用，发帖暂不会被自动归档或分析。

The archive is the system's sole source of truth: save messages and revisions first, then analyze the saved records. Summaries and issues are derived results. The archive bot and AI processing are not installed or enabled on this server yet; posts are not currently archived or analyzed automatically.

计划中的归档范围是公开文字频道、论坛和主题串；工作人员私密区及私信不在范围内。访问权限、保留期限以及编辑/删除记录的处理规则需在归档启用前确认。请只分享适合公开 Discord 社区的内容，不要发敏感个人信息。

The planned scope is public text channels, forums, and threads; private staff areas and DMs are excluded. Access, retention periods, and handling of edits/deletions must be settled before archiving is enabled. Share only what is suitable for a public Discord community; do not post sensitive personal data.

私下求助可联系服主 Aegleseeker 或可见工作人员；若私信不可达，在 🙋・新手求助-help 仅留下“需要私下协助”，不要公开细节。私密对话由工作人员按需人工安排。不要发送密码、登录令牌或完整存档。

For private help, contact owner Aegleseeker or visible staff. If DMs are unavailable, request private assistance in 🙋・新手求助-help without posting details. Staff arrange restricted conversations manually. Never send passwords, login tokens, or complete save files.
